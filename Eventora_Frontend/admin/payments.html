Vendor Net

How this is calculated. commission_amount = total_amount × 10.00% and vendor net = total_amount − commission_amount. Vendor net is derived in the query and never stored, so it cannot drift from the commission record. The rate is snapshotted on each booking when it is placed. Only completed bookings appear — money exists once the service is delivered.
