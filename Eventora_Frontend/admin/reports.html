Financial overview

Commission records
Source of these figures. Gross is the sum of bookings.total_amount for completed bookings. Commission is the sum of commissions.commission_amount — the authoritative table — not a percentage applied to a total here. Vendor net is derived as gross minus commission. Only completed bookings contribute: money exists once the service is delivered.
