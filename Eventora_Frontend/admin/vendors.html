Verification and account management

EA Eventora Admin Administrator

Demo vendor directory. All 20 vendors from the public marketplace, with counts derived from the same dataset. Approving, rejecting or blocking changes this page only — nothing is saved and no vendor is notified.

Verification and account status are two independent fields. vendor_profiles.is_verified records whether an administrator approved the business documents. vendor_profiles.status records whether the account may trade. Approving documents does not unblock a blocked account, and blocking an account does not revoke verification — a verified vendor can still be blocked. Both are admin-only: a vendor can never self-verify or self-unblock. The two identities are also distinct: vendor_id is the vendor profile, users.id is the account.
