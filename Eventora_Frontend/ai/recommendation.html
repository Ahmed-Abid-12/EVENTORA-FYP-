What the engine will score, and against what. The recommendation target is the service, not the business: recommendations.service_id points at services.service_id, while recommendations.vendor_id is carried alongside so results can be presented as vendor recommendations without a join. Each service will be scored against this event's event_type, event_date, city, guest_count and total_budget, combined with the vendor's avg_rating and the sentiment of their reviews. Public vendor filtering on Find Vendors is not this — that is a SQL WHERE clause and is never labelled AI.
