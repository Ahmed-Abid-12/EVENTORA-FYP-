Why these are separate

Account vs profile

Your account is how you sign in — it is the same whether you use Eventora as a client or, one day, as a vendor. Your client profile holds how you plan events, and it is what bookings, events and reviews are attached to. Keeping them apart is why a vendor and a client can never be confused for one another in the system.
