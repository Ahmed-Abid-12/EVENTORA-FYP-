Privacy

What vendors and other users can see

Show my name on reviews

If off, reviews you write appear without your name attached.

Share event details with booked vendors

Vendors you book see the event date, city, guest count and any special requirements. Turning this off may prevent vendors from quoting accurately.

Vendors only ever see details for bookings you have made with them. They cannot browse client accounts. Administrators can access account records to manage the platform and resolve disputes. Like the notification toggles above, these switches have no column in Database v1.0 yet.

Account status

Deactivating is reversible — deleting is not offered

Deactivate my account

Your account is set to blocked and you can no longer sign in. Nothing is deleted: your bookings, payments, commission records and reviews are kept, because vendors and the platform need that history for accounting. Any booking a vendor has already accepted must be settled with that vendor first.
