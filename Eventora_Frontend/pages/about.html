About Eventora

Event planning, without the guesswork.

We are building a centralised platform that connects people planning an event with the local vendors who can deliver it — and uses AI to make that match a good one.

Eventora is an AI-powered event management platform. It brings clients and event-service vendors — caterers, decorators, photographers, venues, mehndi artists, DJs and event planners — into one place, from first search through to booking, payment and review.

The platform is being developed as a Final Year Project in the Department of Information Technology at the University of Sindh, Jamshoro, under Project ID SU|FET|IT|FYP|2026. It is designed from the start as a commercial product rather than a coursework exercise: the architecture, the database and the business model are all built to run in the real world.

Our focus is deliberately local. Existing platforms concentrate on Karachi and Lahore, which leaves Hyderabad, Jamshoro and the surrounding districts of Sindh without a serious digital option. That gap is where Eventora starts.

We are not just a directory. We are an intelligent event planning co-pilot.
Eventora project proposal, 2026
The Problem

Why planning an event locally is still so hard.

These six issues were identified during the research phase of our project and are what the platform is built to address.

Search takes days, not minutes

Finding and comparing vendors means phone calls, physical visits and scattered WhatsApp threads — slow, stressful and easy to get wrong.

No budget-aware filtering

Existing platforms cannot recommend vendors against a real budget, so people either overspend or settle for the wrong service.

Too many reviews to read

Hundreds of unstructured reviews per vendor are impossible to process by hand, so genuine reliability stays hidden.

No planning assistance

No local platform offers real-time guidance to collect requirements and walk someone through their decisions.

Secondary cities are ignored

Platforms operate in a handful of major cities. Hyderabad, Jamshoro and the rest of Sindh are left with offline methods.

No transparent ranking

Without data-driven ranking there is no reliable way to judge vendor quality, responsiveness or value for money.

Our Mission

To remove the manual effort from event planning by giving every client intelligent, budget-aware vendor recommendations — and to give local vendors a digital storefront that reaches customers word of mouth never could.

Our Vision

To become Pakistan's largest AI-powered event ecosystem, where planning an event becomes as straightforward as booking a ride online — starting in Hyderabad, expanding across Sindh, and then nationwide.

What We Are Building

Six systems, one platform.

AI Vendor Recommendation

A Python recommendation engine scores every service against your event — its type, date, city, guest count and budget — alongside vendor rating and review sentiment, then returns a ranked shortlist grouped by vendor.

  • Scores services, presents vendors
  • Budget compatibility is a ranking factor, not a filter
  • Requires a signed-in client and a created event

AI Chatbot

A conversational assistant that collects your requirements, answers questions and guides you through planning decisions in real time.

Review Summarisation

NLP condenses hundreds of reviews per vendor into a readable summary with a sentiment score, so reliability is visible at a glance.

Budget Estimation

Cost estimation that spreads your total budget sensibly across venue, food, decoration and the rest — before you commit to anything.

Vendor Marketplace

Verified local vendors with service listings, pricing, capacity and portfolio galleries across all seven categories.

Booking, Payment & Commission

A complete booking lifecycle: request, vendor accept or reject, confirmation, secure payment, then a review once the service is delivered. Every booking belongs to an event, so a wedding with five vendors stays one coherent plan instead of five unrelated transactions. Eventora takes a platform commission on each completed booking — vendors keep the rest.

  • EasyPaisa, JazzCash, bank transfer and card
  • Commission rate recorded at booking time, so history stays auditable
  • Separate dashboards for clients, vendors and admin
