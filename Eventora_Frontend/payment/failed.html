What a failed payment does and does not change. payments.status is the transaction outcome — that is what failed. bookings.status is the booking state — it stays accepted, so you can try again. And no commissions record is created, because a commission only exists where money was actually earned. These are three different things and a failure touches only the first.

Common reasons a payment fails

1

Insufficient balance, or a daily transaction limit on the account.

2

The confirmation was cancelled, or the session timed out before it completed.

3

A temporary problem at the payment provider. Trying a different method often works.
