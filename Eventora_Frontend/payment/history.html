Payment status is not booking status. payments.status is the transaction outcome — one of pending, success, failed, refunded. bookings.status is a separate five-value enum, and the commissions record is a third thing again: what the platform earned. Paying does not complete a booking — the vendor marks it completed after delivering the service. No payout status is shown anywhere, because Database v1.0 does not model one.
